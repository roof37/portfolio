Overview

For my high school English class in my senior year, we had to give a 10-minute TED Talk style presentation on whatever we wanted. This was the presentation I ended up presenting.

What it covers

The presentation opens with a statistical breakdown of Michael Jordan’s quote about missed shots and lost games, then looks at times to ignore statistical data and the “eye test.” From there it covers Moneyball and the Oakland Athletics, recruiting strategy that relies heavily on statistics, and two examples of players defying their odds. It also draws on insights from Dr. Bryan Nelson, a statistics professor at the University of Pittsburgh (read the interview).
