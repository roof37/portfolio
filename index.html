Penn State Men's Soccer - Co-Lead Performance Analyst Jan 2026 – Present

I work with the coaching staff on the data side of the program. I co-built a dashboard that pulls event data from Hudl Statsbomb and Wyscout into one central place, so the staff can see team information without hunting through separate tools. Week to week, I scout high school, international and Transfer Portal players and write up what stands out for the coaches to evaluate. Before matches, I put together opponent reports on the threats we'll face and where they're vulnerable, and I cut film on opposing attackers so our defenders can study their habits and likely matchups.

Ken Clark Speed - Sprints Biomechanics Intern Jun 2026 – Present

I work alongside Dr. Ken Clark breaking down sprint film from USATF meets. Using Kinovea, I mark up races frame by frame to study how elite sprinters move, then track the results so athletes can see what the film shows. At live meets, the work moves fast: results have to be turned around between rounds so athletes can adjust before their next race. I also stay in close contact with the other analysts to cross-check our markings so the whole team's results are consistent and reliable.

Penn State Men's Lacrosse - Lead Data Analyst Sep 2024 – May 2026

I founded the program's analytics department and led a small team of analysts. We studied film of opponents and our own team to find the trends that mattered, and used Python and Excel to turn them into shot plots and other visuals the coaches could use. I worked directly with the coaching staff to set and prioritize the analytical workload each week through two seasons, so the work always fed into how they were preparing for games.

Research

The Suzy and Jim Broadhurst Golf Teaching and Research Center - Golf Biomechanics Research Assistant May 2026 – Present

I'm helping build an interactive web application that models golf swing biomechanics and ball flight, working with Dr. Eric Handley, Dr. Joe LeSueur and Dan Merrell. It's designed as a teaching aid for Penn State World Campus' course Biomechanics of Golf in the Penn State College of Health and Human Development, so the goal is to make the physics easy to see and explore. I built the simulation on TrackMan launch monitor data, showing how swing path, club face angle and ball contact interact to shape where the ball ends up.
